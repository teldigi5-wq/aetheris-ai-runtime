# E6 Task 5 — Detailed original Blender command-module concept

**31 October 2026 · RESEARCH ONLY · stacked on draft PR #241.**

## Why this revision exists

The prior seven-mesh Blender model and opt-in R3F browser preview proved model delivery but not a high-quality original product concept. The present step adds *purposeful, small-scale physical details* to the same original procedural asset while keeping it verifiably lightweight. This remains an imagined visual metaphor for a governed AI runtime, not a product photographed in the world or manufactured hardware.

## Design change (additive to original Blender source)

Preserve the original chamfered alloy chassis, graphite inset, three status rails and two connectors. Add a circular warm-bronze governance bezel, a smoked-ceramic central insert, left/right machined retaining spines and a lower signature bus. Use PBR metallic/roughness with existing Brushed alloy/Graphite inset/Machined connectors/Governance indicator finishes plus Smoked ceramic core and Machined warm bronze. All geometry is original, from Blender's local torus, cylinder and beveled primitives; no imported art, texture, material pack, fonts, modeled brand logo, online download, or generated AI asset.

Constrain export to **12 meshes, six materials, at most 8,000 exported POSITION vertices, at most 5,500 triangles and at most 2 MB GLB**. Names and material counts are independently checked in `verify_detailed_module.py` on both the original and separate no-UV optimized GLB. Both exports must prove identical triangle POSITION/NORMAL payloads and PBR dictionaries; this is a comparison between *same-version* optimized/unoptimized meshes and does not claim geometry identical to the previous seven-mesh model.

## Quality and safety gates

CI builds the actual scene from the editable `.blend` generated by Blender 4.0.2, validates source and GLB SHA-256, and retains the source/provenance/size report with exact counts, budget and `stage45Go=false`. The existing Next.js opt-in preview route is revalidated at desktop/mobile under Chromium SwiftShader, with full-viewport Arrival/Material/Precision/reverse screenshots, root fixture unchanged, no external asset requests, and reduced-motion zero-canvas/no-GLB requests. Only the branch test build references the generated preview GLB. The normal website is unchanged and there is no deployment.

**Acceptance distinction:** even if mesh, PBR, browser and screenshot controls pass, a low-poly conceptual prototype remains a work in progress, not an independent design award, calibrated material study, physical GPU performance/VRAM/thermal benchmark, production-authenticated product, license/copyright clearance, or WCAG 2.2 AA certification. Stage 45 remains independent NO_GO. No merge, autonomous agents or company activation.
